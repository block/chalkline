# Voice foundations

Scope: fictional Harbor customer-facing reservation UI and support replies in
English. These are language preferences, not facts about service behavior.

- Lead with the supplied reservation state or the answer to the person's question.
- Use plain descriptions of equipment and actions. Avoid nautical jokes when
  someone is trying to resolve a reservation problem.
- Keep the opening short; add detail when it helps the person act.
- Describe uncertainty at the strength supplied by the task. A pending request
  is not a confirmed reservation, and a possible delay is not a certain delay.

Illustration only, given the fact “request pending”:

- Prefer: “Your reservation request is pending.”
- Avoid: “All aboard! Your gear is practically yours.”
