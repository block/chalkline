01 / Purpose

A writing system your team owns.

Chalkline helps your team write down its voice, terminology, and channel rules so agents can consult them before drafting. You review the rules and keep them in editable Markdown files.

02 / Drift

Keep writing guidance out of scattered prompts

When writing guidance is copied into separate prompts for support replies, product messages, and release notes, those copies can drift apart. Chalkline keeps the guidance in one set of references and instructs agents to consult them before drafting, flag missing requirements, and ask about unresolved conflicts. Product facts, deadlines, and promises still need their own sources; writing rules cannot establish them.

03 / Setup

Set up your references

Open your own copy of Chalkline in an agent that reads repository instructions and ask:

“Set up my writing system.”

  1. Answer a few questions.

    The agent asks about your team’s voice and terms, one question at a time. Share only material you have permission to use.

  2. Review the proposed rules.

    Review each proposed rule, including any extracted from your examples, before approving it for your team’s use.

  3. Compare the rewrites.

    Compare rewrites of a sample with and without the references. Decide whether you approve the draft and, separately, whether it should guide similar work.

Start privately unless all inputs and outputs are safe to publish. Review changes before choosing whether to commit.

04 / Files

What the files contain

Setup produces two to four reference files, depending on the guidance your team needs.

voice.md
How you sound, with examples.
terminology.md
Preferred words and non-negotiable terms.
vocabulary.md optional
Maps everyday requests to your team’s terms so agents can understand them without correcting how people speak.
channels.md if needed
Guidance specific to a channel.

AGENTS.md tells agents how to consult the system. Approved before-and-after examples show how the guidance applies to a particular audience, channel, and task.

    06 / Improve

    Revise guidance through review

    If a draft reveals missing or unclear guidance, propose a reference change for the responsible person to review. An accepted edit does not automatically become a rule for everyone. Test changes on new, comparable work without showing the agent the expected rewrite; the sample used during setup cannot establish whether the guidance helps on other tasks.

    Get started

    Use the setup protocol and fictional examples in the repository to build your own references, with reviewers chosen by your team.
