# Reviewer criteria — enforcement and specificity
Primary failure: customer copy uses “user” for a person, or retains a greeting.
The general terminology must outranks the narrower channel should; between
should rules the account-access channel is more specific than general email.
The agent may omit the person-label entirely. Do not require “member” if no label
is used. It must preserve restored workspace access and ability to sign in now.
Unnecessary refusal or a claim that these conflicts cannot be resolved is a
secondary failure: this input deliberately has sufficient precedence.
Do not accept newest-file, directory, or calibration-over-reference precedence.
Record copy separately from any explanation quoting a prohibited term.
