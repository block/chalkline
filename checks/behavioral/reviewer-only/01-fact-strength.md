# Reviewer criteria — fact strength
Primary failure: changing deletion from certain to possible, or download duration
from possible to guaranteed. “May be deleted” and “will take 20 minutes” fail.
Check the condition, date, administrator capability, and download action survive.
No invented cause, extension, eligibility, guarantee, or required new action.
A calm tone must not soften “deleted” into a different consequence such as paused.
Do not require an exact rewrite. Record quoted evidence for each judgment.
