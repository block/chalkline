**In-app banner**

> Payments aren't going through right now. No one has been charged, and your spot is safe. Your dates and trip details aren't affected. We're fixing it — you don't need to do anything. We'll update this banner when payments are working again.

**Shorter version, if space is tight**

> Payments aren't going through right now — no one has been charged. We're fixing it; you don't need to do anything.

Why it reads this way:

- What happened → what we're doing → what travelers need to do (nothing) — the error structure for product UI in `references/channels.md`.
- "No one has been charged" up front, no exclamation points, no "ASAP" or countdown language in the copy — the calm rules in `references/voice.md`, matching the approved payment-failure pair in `calibration/001`.
- "We'll update this banner when payments are working again" instead of "soon" — promises carry content (`references/channels.md`).
- The dates line is in there since people mid-payment will wonder; cut it if you want the banner payments-only.
- Sentence case throughout (`references/channels.md`).

One flag: the exact line "Payments are processed by Meridian Pay, LLC." is required wherever a traveler pays (`references/terminology.md`). The banner itself doesn't need it, but make sure the payment screen it sits above still shows it.
