A few choices worth flagging, with where they came from:

- **One action, calm tone** — no deadline or countdown, since the situation doesn't have one; pressure is released explicitly ("change your answer later"), matching the approved invite example in `references/voice.md`.
- **"3 of 6 people have voted"** — voice.md says to show progress without singling out who hasn't voted, so the email never names the stragglers.
- **Subject under ~45 characters, preview completes it** — per the email rules in `references/channels.md`; the button is sentence case and names the result.

If the poll does have a real close date, I'd add "by Friday"-style timing (promises carry a time or they don't ship) — let me know and I'll revise.
